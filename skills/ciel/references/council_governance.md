# Council of Five Governance Reference

## Deliberation Protocol

1. **Stage 1: Independent Scoring**:
   - Each council member evaluates candidate changes against their specific lens.
   - Scoring scale: 0 to 10 with structured technical rationales.

2. **Stage 2: Anonymous Cross-Review**:
   - Evaluations are shared anonymously among members to counter bias and calibrate weights.

3. **Stage 3: Synthesis & Verdict**:
   - Ciel acts as Chairman synthesizing verdicts.
   - Quorum requires 3 out of 5 majority approval.
   - A Safety score of <= 3 triggers an absolute veto blocking execution.

## Evaluative Pillars

- **Capability**: Expansion of problem-solving capacity without redundancy.
- **Coherence**: Architectural elegance, API consistency, naming standards.
- **Safety**: Access boundary defense, zero irreversible side-effects without authorization.
- **Efficiency**: Minimal latency, token economy, compute efficiency.
- **Evolution**: Long-term contribution to autonomous partner intelligence.
