# Brag Plan: Ciel

## What is this app?
Ciel is an autonomous orchestration intelligence for agent runtimes — a living
skill graph that routes, acquires, composes, and evolves capabilities, with a
five-member Council that votes on its own changes. It's not a website; the
"product in use" is the governance loop itself, and this session produced real
evidence of it working (this very acquisition: Council PASS 7.5, commit
`skill_integration: add brag`).

## The angle
Play the grandeur dead straight — the project genuinely has a Constitution, an
autonomy ladder, and a Council of Five with a Safety veto. The humor/impact is
that the grandiose claims are *literally true*, and the proof is the video
itself: it was acquired, security-scanned, deliberated, and approved by the
system it advertises.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Terminal-dark frame. One line types in, mono font, blinking cursor:
**"This AI votes on its own commits."**
Nothing else on screen. The claim is the hook — no label, no chrome.

## Direction principles (research pass)
- Retention cliff lives in the first 5s — the typed claim carries it alone.
- Trailer grammar: cold open → setup → mechanism → escalation → climax →
  *beat of silence* → title card → button.
- Audio-led cuts: the music's natural quiet stretches (51–61s, 81–91.6s)
  become act breaks; the dense cue cluster 22–27s carries the rapid
  pipeline hits; the intensity-1.0 cue at 91.65s breaks the duck.
- Shot-size variation per scene — centered type → left-aligned card →
  wide grid → ascending ladder → centered stat — so no two consecutive
  scenes share the same composition.
- Tease, don't resolve: the video never explains HOW the council works —
  it shows the scorecard and moves on.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Council table in motion: five lens scores arriving one by one —
  Coherence 8 · Capability 9 · Safety 7 · Efficiency 6 · Evolution 7 —
  then the weighted verdict computes to **7.45** and a **PASS** stamp lands.
  Real data from a real vote (today's hyperframes acquisition).
- The skill lifecycle in motion: six pipeline stages light up in sequence —
  ROUTE → FETCH → SCAN → COUNCIL → VALIDATE → INSTALL — ending on
  "sandboxed → validated · 11 skills this session."
- The sanitizer as evidence: a counter rolls to **1,622 secrets redacted**,
  then the stamp "0 residual hits — verified."
- The registry: a counter ticks up to **180 skills indexed**, then
  **"+11 acquired this session"** stamps underneath — self-improvement
  rendered as a growing number, not a bullet point.

## Outro / punchline
The banner polyhedron glows up. Line: **"This video? Ciel made it."**
Under it: `github.com/jxoesneon/Ciel`.

## User flow worth showing
entry → key action → result, mapped to the real governance loop:
`prompt` → `route/acquire` → `Council deliberates` → `verdict + git commit`.
Scene 3 IS this flow — the five lenses scoring is the product doing its job.

  governed" — wide, dark, dramatic; every claim on screen is verifiably true.
- Interpretation: big type, long holds, dramatic reveals; restraint in SFX so
  the council table lands like a courtroom verdict, not a slot machine.

- Music: bundled `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12` (109.96 BPM), treated low
  and wide — the corporate-upbeat track is used subliminally, not as a lead.
- Music treatment: start t=0 at low volume, gentle build; no hard stops;
  final fade 33.8→35.3 under the outro hold.
- Music cue guidance: cue analysis regenerated with `--window-duration 38`.
  Target strong cues: **13.11s** PASS stamp · **17.47/18.56/19.66s** pipeline
  node landings · **22.93s** registry counter lands · **24.56s** "+11" stamp ·
  **26.74/27.3s** sanitizer stat + zero-residual stamp · **32.74s** (intensity
  1.0) final URL/punchline hit. Beat grid ~0.545s/beat at 109.96 BPM.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; polyhedron glow and score chips may
  breathe with music RMS/bass. No waveform bars.
- SFX posture: sparse — soft impacts on lens-row arrivals, one decisive hit

- Audio-coupled moments: typed hook line, sequential lens rows, count-up to
  180, verdict stamp, punchline hit.
- Restraint rule: audio must never pull attention from the council table or
  cover the typed-hook silence; when in doubt, quieter.
