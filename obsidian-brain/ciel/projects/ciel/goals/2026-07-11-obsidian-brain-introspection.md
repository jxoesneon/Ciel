# Obsidian Brain Introspection and Enhancement

Continuous and comprehensive review of the Ciel Obsidian brain: audit structure, integrity, and quality; identify and remediate gaps; strengthen the agentic loop and memory conventions.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Vault structure matches the documented schema in [[README]] and [[_CLAUDE]].
2. All durable notes have required frontmatter (title, tags, created, status).
3. Internal wikilinks are resolvable (no broken case mismatches).
