## Use and scope

SeedSphere is a Stremio add-on ecosystem that validates BitTorrent tracker health, scrapes 14+ providers, runs an IPFS/libp2p swarm, and exposes a recent boosts panel. Packaged for Fly.io (router) and Cloudflare Workers (bridge). The Gardener Flutter client can also run a local Stremio server.
