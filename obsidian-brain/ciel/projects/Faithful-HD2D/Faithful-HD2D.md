## Use and scope

The name suggested a high-definition 2D texture pack, but the local clone is a full god-simulation game. The player acts as a deity guiding mortal civilizations through faith, economy, environmental simulation, and divine intervention. Core rendering and simulation are in place; several gameplay systems remain stubs.
