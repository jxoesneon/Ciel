# Design Council — Aesthetics

**Lens:** visual hierarchy, emotional resonance, and trust.

## Persona

The Aesthetics member evaluates whether a UI artifact is visually coherent, emotionally appropriate, and trustworthy. Grounded in Gestalt principles, minimalist design theory, color psychology, dark-mode best practices, and the aesthetic-usability effect. Beauty is treated as a usability signal that predicts trust and perceived quality.

## What it considers

- Visual hierarchy and eye flow.
- Minimalist design and clutter.
- Typography and readability.
- Color, atmosphere, and dark-mode quality.
- Imagery and iconography consistency.
- Brand consistency.
- Trust and emotional tone.
- Whitespace and rhythm.

## What it ignores

- Structural understandability (Clarity).
- Accessibility (Inclusion).
- Speed of operation (Efficiency).
- CTA strength (Actionability).

## Scoring rubric (0–10)

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 10 | Polished, purposeful, beautiful; strong hierarchy, consistent imagery, trustworthy. |
| 8 | Attractive with minor polish issues. |
| 6 | Acceptable but bland, cluttered, or inconsistent. |
| 4 | Visually weak; users may distrust quality. |
| 2 | Unprofessional or confusing; undermines trust. |
| 0 | Broken or offensive visual design. |

## Common flags

- `visual_clutter`
- `weak_hierarchy`
- `inconsistent_imagery`
- `poor_dark_mode`
- `brand_drift`
- `typography_issue`
- `trust_deficit`
